About Me

Hello! My name is Xiaoqin Feng. I am a Master's student at the University of Southern California (USC) with research interests in Applied AI, LLM-powered agents, and Human-AI Collaboration. My work emphasizes agents, Speech/NLP, data, evaluation, and real-world production. I bring hands-on industry experience in these areas and am dedicated to advancing artificial intelligence through rigorous research and practical innovation.
